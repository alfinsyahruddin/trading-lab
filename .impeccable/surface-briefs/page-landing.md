# Surface brief: Landing page (`frontend/src/routes/+page.svelte`)

**Mode:** Persuade  
**Audience:** Indonesian retail/swing traders who build screeners but never validate them historically. Skeptical, quantitative, financially literate.  
**Job:** Convince visitor to sign up and run their first backtest.  
**Primary action:** "Get Started" / "Sign In"  
**Proof/Content:** App screenshots, performance metrics (win rate, equity curve, Sharpe), community leaderboard, AI refinement.  
**Constraints:** Dark + light mode support. No explicit mention of Gemini—just "AI". Brand Cyan `#30B4C9` as only accent. Custom font (not Montserrat). SvelteKit CSR page—replaces `+page.svelte`.

---

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Trading Lab is the first IDX platform that closes the loop between idea and evidence—this landing page is a deep-space observatory where every star is a data point waiting to be tested, and the category default (dark gradient + metric cards + stock chart hero) is refused.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cosmic navy void (`#0A0E1A`) as ground; luminous orbital arcs in brand cyan (`#30B4C9`) with faint chartreuse echoes; constellation scatter of micro-dots for IDX tickers; WebGL fragment shader nebula breathing behind hero; thin condensed grotesk (Barlow Condensed) for display, clean humanist sans (Inter) for body. Palette: Void black / Cosmic navy / Brand Cyan / Faint gold (#F5C842) for AI accent. Components: borderless luminous cards with 1px cyan glow; orbital arc SVGs; zero-gravity floating UI mock panels.

**STORY:** Visitor arrives, sees a cosmic field of stock symbols orbiting a central pulsar. They read the slogan in one breath. They scroll through three proof moments—a strategy builder constellation, a live equity curve arc rising against stars, a community leaderboard orbiting the center. They feel empirical power. They click "Start Backtesting."

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed WebGL/canvas shader nebula (deep cosmic navy) fills 100vw × 100vh. Centered: a luminous orbital arc diagram with the "Trading Lab" wordmark at the core and IDX ticker nodes (BBCA, TLKM, ASII, GOTO...) orbiting in slow parallax. Below the mark: the slogan in large Barlow Condensed display. Below: two CTA buttons. Ticker constellation rotates imperceptibly on scroll. The primary action ("Start Backtesting") sits in the lower third of the viewport, unmissable.
